# Four Mobile Wedding Designs

## Goal

Create four clearly different, polished mobile-first visual directions for the existing wedding invitation without changing its content, links, media, interactions, or currently published version.

## Safety and navigation

- The existing presentation remains available at `index.html?design=original`.
- New directions use `index.html?design=a`, `b`, `c`, and `d`.
- `preview.html` is a local design switcher showing all five links.
- No deployment is performed until Pavel chooses a direction.

## Shared requirements

- Primary viewport: 360–430 CSS pixels.
- Preserve all wedding information, photos, music, calendar action, map links, Telegram confirmation, decline video, and animations.
- Increase body-text contrast, remove excessive empty space, keep buttons readable, and prevent the music control from covering content.
- Motion must support `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Directions

### A — Editorial Ivory

Refined evolution of the current design: ivory paper, espresso typography, restrained script accents, thin rules, clean timeline, and layered monochrome photographs.

### B — Cinematic

Dark cocoa and black surfaces, full-bleed monochrome photographs, ivory type, high contrast, dramatic pacing, and minimal framing.

### C — Romantic Album

Warm paper, tactile photo stacks, handwritten captions, irregular rotations, soft shadows, compact blocks, and an intimate scrapbook rhythm.

### D — Modern Minimal

Strict grid, oversized dates and times, sans-serif body copy, crisp cards, strong hierarchy, minimal decoration, and maximum readability.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each direction is visually recognizable within the first screen.
- Schedule, details, route, dress code, calendar, and RSVP remain usable on mobile.
- No direction relies on tiny low-contrast text.
- The original remains directly accessible and unchanged by theme overrides.
